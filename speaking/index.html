Speaking

Agent governance, confidential computing, and what a system can actually prove.

What I speak about

I speak about building and operating AI systems, and the security and standards needed to use them responsibly. My talks draw on engineering work and contributions across AAIF, Appia, and CoSAI, with practical examples of what works, what fails, and what remains unproven.

Building AI systems for production

Architecture, reliability, and operating AI at scale: from models and agent workflows to tools, data, and infrastructure. Practical lessons on deployment, evaluation, observability, and the trade-offs teams face in production.

AI security, governance, and open standards

How organizations control AI systems and assess the evidence behind them. Topics include agent identity and interoperability, authorization, confidential computing, model custody, auditability, and turning risk-management requirements into engineering controls.

Author of Architecting at Scale (Packt, 2026), a field guide to large-scale AI-native system design.
