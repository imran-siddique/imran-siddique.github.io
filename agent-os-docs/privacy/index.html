Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 30, 2026

Summary: AgentOS collects minimal data necessary to provide the service. We don't sell your data, and we prioritize your privacy and security.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how AgentOS ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our GitHub Copilot Extension and related services (collectively, the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • GitHub Account Information: When you install AgentOS, we receive your GitHub username and basic profile information through GitHub's OAuth flow.
  • Agent Code: Code you create or modify using AgentOS is processed to provide our services but is not stored permanently on our servers.
  • Chat Messages: Messages you send to @agentos in GitHub Copilot Chat are processed to generate responses.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We collect anonymized usage statistics such as which commands are used most frequently.
  • Error Logs: When errors occur, we collect diagnostic information to improve the service.
  • Performance Metrics: Response times and system performance data.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your private repository contents (unless explicitly shared via a command)
  • Your GitHub credentials or tokens
  • Personal information beyond what GitHub provides
  • Payment or financial information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the AgentOS service
  • Generate agent code based on your natural language descriptions
  • Validate code against compliance frameworks
  • Detect and prevent security issues
  • Respond to your requests and provide support
  • Send service-related communications

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing
  • Service Providers: With vendors who help us operate our service (hosting, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • GitHub: As necessary to integrate with GitHub Copilot

5. Data Retention

  • Chat Messages: Processed in real-time and not stored after response generation
  • Usage Analytics: Retained for 90 days in anonymized form
  • Error Logs: Retained for 30 days
  • Account Information: Retained while your installation is active

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
  • Encryption at rest (AES-256)
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure development practices

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data by uninstalling the app
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@agent-os.dev

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

9. Children's Privacy

AgentOS is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice in the Service or sending you an email.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@agent-os.dev
  • GitHub Issues: github.com/imran-siddique/agent-os/issues

12. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal basis for processing includes:

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide the Service
  • Legitimate Interests: Improving our service and preventing abuse
  • Consent: Where you have given explicit consent
